👋 About Me

Hi, I'm Chris! I'm a software engineer at ASML Brion in San Jose, CA working on quality and developer tooling for the Tachyon platform and pattern technology. I graduated from UC Berkeley with a degree in Computer Science.

I discovered my love for solving quality problems during college through unit testing and TDD, but realized that the real impact comes from building tools that scale solutions across teams. I'm obsessed with the intersection of rigorous testing, clean architecture, and automation that empowers engineers rather than constraining them. This led me to create documentation-first tools and infrastructure that make complex systems more reliable and trustworthy.

At ASML, I've ranked in the top 3 of my department for bug verification and owned multiple tools adopted by 60+ engineers—from diagnostic optimizations reducing runtime from 17 hours to 10 minutes, to Python I/O tracking systems with full documentation and iterative refinement. I expanded GPU test coverage 20x and drove 100% regression tracking across 800+ test cases. Each project taught me that the best quality infrastructure combines precision engineering with usability.

💡 What Drives Me

I'm driven by solving the hardest parts of complex systems—whether that's reliability, performance, or making the invisible visible. ASML's machines are the most complex in the world, and it falls to us to build tools and testing infrastructure that catch problems before they reach customers. For me, it's not just about bugs; it's about creating infrastructure people trust and want to use.

I love the challenge of building solutions that work across disciplines: test frameworks that developers love, tools that make automation intuitive, and systems designed from first principles. That blend—precision engineering plus developer empathy—is where I find the most impact.
