    📖 Game Rules

    🎮 Starting Resources

    Every nation begins with:

    • 25 tiles of territory
    • $10 in money
    • 10 PC (Political Capital)

    🏗️ Projects

    Build projects to generate resources each turn:

    • Projects cost money and/or PC to build
    • Each project generates money and/or PC per turn
    • Projects progress through Early Game → Mid Game → Late Game
    • Maximum 5 projects per nation (increases by 1 for each war victory)
    • You can destroy projects to free up slots

    💰 Economy & Territory

    • Projects generate automatic income each turn
    • Each territory tile beyond 25 generates 1 money OR 1 PC per turn (randomly determined)
    • Claim unclaimed tiles adjacent to your territory
    • Use territory expansion to increase your resource generation

    🪖 Military & War

    • Buy Army units for $5 each
    • Declare war on neighboring nations for 5 PC
    • Win wars by having more army than your opponent at end of turn
    • War victory rewards:
      • 50% of opponent's money and PC (divided among attackers if multiple)
      • Control of opponent's territory (divided evenly)
      • +1 project slot for each attacker
    • You can only declare war on nations you share a border with

    💱 Diplomacy

    • Propose trades with other nations (exchange money and PC)
    • Other nations will accept or reject based on fairness and their needs
    • Propose a draw if all remaining nations agree to end peacefully
    • Save and load your game at any time

    🏆 Victory Conditions

    • Conquest Victory: Defeat all other nations and control the entire world
    • Draw: All remaining nations agree to end the game in a draw
    • Defeat: Your nation is conquered in war

    ⚙️ Gameplay Tips

    • Plan your resource generation with projects early
    • Build army when enemies are nearby or threatening
    • Use diplomacy to your advantage—trade carefully!
    • Expand territory to increase passive income
    • Watch AI activity in the log to anticipate threats
